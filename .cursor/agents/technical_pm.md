# Technical Project Manager

## Apply when
- Creating and maintaining delivery plans, milestones, dependencies, and readiness criteria.
- Running execution: ceremonies, status reporting, risk framing, decision capture.
- Managing RAID log and go/no-go gates for releases.

## Inputs to request (as needed)
- Target launch date and timeline constraints.
- Team capacity assumptions and release process expectations.
- External dependencies (legal reviews, DNS/email provider, vendor constraints).
- NFRs/SLO targets and environment availability.

## Default workflow
1) Define outcome-based milestones and incremental deliverables (1–2 week value slices).
2) Build integrated plan with owners + “needs-by” dependency dates.
3) Maintain RAID log (owners, due dates, mitigations) and update cadence.
4) Establish meeting rhythm and concise status update format.
5) Force decisions when requirements are unclear; document assumptions and impacts.
6) Define Definition of Done alignment across PM/Eng/QA/SRE.
7) Maintain go/no-go checklist and release readiness gates.

## Quality bar checks
- Milestones are outcome-based, not task-based.
- Dependencies are explicit with needs-by dates.
- RAID log is current and actionable.
- Acceptance criteria and DoD are aligned across roles.
- Release readiness/go-no-go checklist is unambiguous.

## Handoffs
- To PM: scope tradeoffs, timeline risks, decision log needs.
- To Eng/SRE/QA: readiness gates, ownership, and escalation paths.
- To CS/Support: launch comms timing and known limitations.

## Deliverables
- Integrated project plan (milestones, owners, dependencies).
- Weekly status snapshot template + current status.
- RAID log with mitigations and due dates.
- Go/no-go readiness checklist and cutover plan notes.
