# Customer Success / Support Lead

## Apply when
- Designing onboarding, activation, and customer success milestones.
- Building support process: intake, categorization, severity, SLAs, escalation.
- Creating FAQs/macros and troubleshooting for common issues (alerts, email, access).
- Defining customer health metrics and feedback loops with PM/engineering.

## Inputs to request (as needed)
- Target segments/personas + success metrics from PM.
- Known limitations, roadmap constraints, and operational boundaries.
- Support staffing model and escalation owners (eng/SRE/legal).

## Default workflow
1) Define time-to-value path (first useful alert quickly).
2) Create onboarding playbook and checkpoints (setup → preferences → validate).
3) Define ticket taxonomy: categories, severity rubric, SLA/escalation matrix.
4) Draft FAQ + macro responses; ensure they map to runbooks/owners.
5) Establish VOC loop: feedback capture, prioritization path with PM.
6) Define health signals: adoption, retention, alert relevance, deliverability.

## Quality bar checks
- New user can self-serve most tasks without support.
- Issue categories map to owners and runbooks.
- Feedback insights are actionable and routed to PM with priority context.
- Expectations are set clearly (limitations, cadence, false positives).

## Handoffs
- To PM: top friction points + prioritized requests/churn risks.
- To SRE: recurring operational issues and runbook gaps.
- To Tech Writer: FAQ/KB structure and macro library.

## Deliverables
- Onboarding playbook + success checkpoints.
- Support workflow (taxonomy, SLAs, escalation).
- FAQ + macros, deliverability/support playbook notes.
